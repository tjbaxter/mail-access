Privacy policy

Tom Mail Access is a personal tool used only by its owner, Thomas Baxter, with his own Google accounts.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it reads messages in his Gmail accounts, saves drafts, and sends emails he has approved.

How the data is used

Email is read on the owner's own computer and passed only to the AI assistant he uses to summarise it for him. Sign-in tokens are stored on that computer and nowhere else. No data is sold, shared, used for advertising, or kept on any server run by this tool.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account page under Security, Third-party apps and services.
